      How a picture becomes a model

      From one image to full 3D

      Every model here starts with a single image that I direct, so the AI gets a clean, well-lit subject to work from.

      1. Directed referenceA studio reference made with Nano Banana 2.1: one subject, soft even light, full body in a relaxed A-pose for people.
      2. AI sculpt & textureMeshy 7.1 builds the Ultra 4K mesh and paints 8K PBR textures: colour, roughness, metal and surface detail.
      3. Built for the webI compress each model (Draco geometry, 4K WebP textures) so it loads fast in any browser, from 2 to 9 MB.

      Lina and John are AI-generated characters, not real people. Lina and my own portrait bust are modelled straight from close-up portraits for an exact likeness. All models are my own work and are shown for portfolio purposes only.
