    How a face becomes a film

    From a photo to a finished scene

    This is why every character on this site is original. The people are real, the AI character is my own, and every frame is directed by me.

    1. Reference, with consentI start from a photo the person has agreed to share, or from a character I designed, like Jane.
    2. Character sheetI build front, side and full-body references so the face stays consistent across every shot.
    3. Directed & finishedI write the scene, generate and pick the shots, then edit, grade and score the final film.
