03 / Process

How I build a publication

The challenge

Organise dense information without overwhelming the reader, balance authority with an approachable style, and stay consistent across articles and page types.

My contribution

Editorial planning, grid development, layout, typography, image treatment and production.

Tools & techniques

Adobe InDesign · Modular grids · Typographic hierarchy · Publication templates · Colour-consistent image editing · Preflight and export.

  1. 1 · Content audit

    Review articles, images, priorities and production needs.

  2. 2 · Information architecture

    Group content and map the reading sequence.

  3. 3 · Visual direction

    Typography, grid, colour and image treatment.

  4. 4 · Layout

    Covers, contents pages and editorial spreads.

  5. 5 · Quality control

    Spacing, alignment, consistency and readability.

  6. 6 · Proofing

    Copy, links, image quality, print and screen behaviour.

  7. 7 · Delivery

    Optimised digital and print-ready files.

  8. Result

    A monthly system that's faster to produce and easier to read.
