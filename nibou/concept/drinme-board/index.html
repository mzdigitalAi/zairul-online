01 · The idea

Every pouch is a passion.

Drinme is a jelly drink you squeeze, slurp and carry anywhere. The pouches already wear hand-drawn street art: guitars, palettes, footballs, boxing gloves. We turn that into one clear promise. Each flavour belongs to a passion, and the drink is the fuel for it.

02 · Logo

One wordmark. Many moods.

We keep the existing drinme wordmark and its ring of dots, redrawn as a clean vector, and give it a system: where it sits, what colour it takes and how it is allowed to move. The ring of dots is the jelly: it becomes our bubble symbol, loader and avatar.

The bubble ring

The dots above the i, pulled out as a symbol. Use it as an avatar, a loader, a sticker, a pattern. Click it: it pops like jelly.

Rules

  • Clear space equal to the height of the d, all round.
  • Minimum size 24 px tall on screen, 12 mm in print.
  • Colours only red, white, ink or yellow, as shown. Never gradients.
  • Never stretch, outline, tilt more than 6° or put it on a busy photo without a flat colour block.

06 · Pouch system

Same shape. Different world.

Every pouch keeps the campaign artwork (illustrated doodles, colour split, hex badge, fruit-shaped Vitamin C seal) with one change: the flavour name is redrawn in clear sticker lettering so it reads at a glance, even small. Move your mouse across them. Click to squeeze.

1 Spout in white. Always clean.
2 Jelly Drink badge. Same spot, every flavour.
3 Name in sticker lettering, tilted.
4 Passion doodles, three per pouch.
5 Vitamin C seal. Same spot, every flavour.

07 · Viral kit

Made to be screenshotted.

Every post follows one template so the feed reads as a single loud family: a flat bold colour, a giant hand-script word behind, the pouch tilted in the middle, big fruit around it, one sticker line, one hashtag and the Nibou badge. It follows the existing campaign look, with the text made easier to read.

09 · New flavour

Meet Passion Fruit.

The flavour that says the tagline out loud. Passion fruit, for the passion nobody has a pouch for yet: gaming. Purple outside, golden inside, with controllers, joysticks and pixel hearts where the other flavours carry guitars and footballs. Switch between flavours to see how the system holds together.

Why this one

  • Built-in pun. "Drink your passion" becomes "Drink your Passion Fruit". Easy to caption, easy to share.
  • Gen Z, finally. Gaming is the biggest passion the range does not cover yet.
  • Looks different on the shelf. Violet is the one colour missing next to orange, aqua and navy.

Taglines

  • Press start. Squeeze.
  • Level up your thirst.
  • Insert coin. Slurp.
  • Player two wants a sip.

Launch moves

  • Limited drop with a scratch-to-reveal high-score code on the pouch.
  • Player Two challenge Swap a pouch with a friend and film the first slurp.
  • Pixel stickers inside the carton: hearts, bolts, controllers.

Lines that work

  • Squish. Slurp. Repeat.
  • Fuel for whatever you are into.
  • Pocket-sized, jelly-sized, you-sized.
  • Minum dulu, hebat kemudian.

Motion principles

Squash 15%, overshoot, settle. 0.5 s.
Dots burst outward, then fade. 0.7 s.
Tilt left, right, rest. Used on hover.
